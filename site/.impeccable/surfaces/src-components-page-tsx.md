# prosed showcase page

Mode: Persuade. One page in five locales, plus a static 404.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves prosed instead of describing it. Every claim sits next to a working miniature of the product: the demo, three looping feature vignettes, real platform and provider logos, and the benchmark numbers. It refuses the text-only long document with hanging chapter heads and an empty left column.

OWN-WORLD: Ink on paper, dark by default with a light toggle. IBM Plex Mono for display and numbers, Plex Sans for UI text. Colour lives only in the product's three marks: mistake red, fix green, rewrite violet. Tiles are hairline-ruled rounded panels on a slightly lifted paper. Logos are monochrome ink-soft.

STORY: The visitor reads "sed for your prose.", watches a message get fixed, sees it runs on their machine and where (macOS, Windows, Chromium browsers), sees the numbers, and downloads the build for their OS.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred h1 at display size, the tagline under it, then a primary ink button "Download for <detected OS>" with the OS logo and a ghost "Source on GitHub". A one-line list of the other downloads sits under them. The compact demo, about 52rem wide, starts above the fold on desktop.

FORM: Proof grid (bento): the roll's lead (index 3 of my ordered list), shown to Florian as option 1 and locked by him; seed key 97eaea09. Signature interaction: three CSS-only vignettes that loop the fix, rewrite and memory behaviours, still under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
